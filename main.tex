\subsection{Discussion}

Our results demonstrate that approximate semantic-geometric
correspondences generated from rigid 3D objects provide effective
supervision for cross-instance matching. KeypointNet evaluations
validate our 3D--3D and projected 3D--2D correspondences,
while SPair-71k results show improvements in real-image
2D--2D matching, including generalization to non-rigid categories. The scalability of our method lies in leveraging large-scale,
approximately aligned 3D collections without manual correspondence
annotations. While alignment remains a prerequisite, existing
datasets such as Objaverse-OA~\cite{orient_anything} and advances
in scalable canonicalization~\cite{jin2025oneshot,jin2026canoverse}
make aligned 3D assets increasingly available.
Moreover, SemGeo-Gen scales combinatorially, generating
correspondences for up to $\binom{N}{2}$ pairs from $N$
aligned objects, with multiple rendered view pairs per pair.
Together, these enable scalable semantic-geometric supervision
without manual point-level annotations.



\subsection{Limitations}

Our approach has several limitations. First, the CPA deformation model cannot fully capture large cross-instance structural differences without supervision, potentially leading to imperfect alignments. Second, our semantic filtering relies on DINOv2 descriptors, although our modular pipeline can benefit from stronger future feature extractors. Third, our method assumes rigid, approximately aligned 3D objects and does not directly handle non-rigid pose variations; a promising extension is to cluster objects by pose and apply CPA within each cluster to accommodate residual variations. Finally, our synthetic 3D assets introduce a domain gap when transferring to real images, although pretraining followed by real-data fine-tuning proves effective. Growing collections of real, canonically aligned 3D assets could further reduce this gap.



\section{Conclusion}

We presented a scalable unsupervised pipeline for generating cross-instance semantic-geometric correspondences from 3D assets without manual annotation. Although approximate, the generated correspondences align well with human annotations and provide an effective pretraining signal for real-image semantic correspondence models. Our results demonstrate that scalable semantic-geometric data can complement manual annotations and improve cross-instance correspondence learning.
